### CHEM-000339: Isomer-resolved organic-nitrate hydrolysis in concentrated aerosol
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Measure individual monoterpene-nitrate hydrolysis and competing reactions in low-water-activity aerosol across acidity, ionic strength and organic matrices.
- **Status:** Seven synthesized standards studied in 2021 showed strongly structure-dependent minute-to-hour lifetimes, with some secondary nitrates resistant under neutral conditions. Actual aerosol matrices remain insufficiently characterized.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Determines whether aerosol nitrates recycle atmospheric nitrogen oxides.
- **Key works:** Y. Wang et al. (2021), *Synthesis and Hydrolysis of Atmospherically Relevant Monoterpene-Derived Organic Nitrates*, [DOI:10.1021/acs.est.1c05310](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c05310).
- **Related:** Atmospheric modeling: nitrogen-oxide recycling from secondary organic aerosol.
